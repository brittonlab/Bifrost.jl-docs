FiberCS

CrossSectionReport
cross_section_report(xs::StepIndexCrossSection; λ_m = 1550e-9, T_K = 297.15)

Eagerly computed optical properties of a StepIndexCrossSection at vacuum wavelength λ_m (metres) and temperature T_K (kelvin).

Fields (SI units):

  • cross_section: the reported cross section.
  • λ_m, T_K: the evaluation point. T_K may carry MCM Particles.

Bifrost.FiberCS._acoustic_mode_terms — Method
_acoustic_mode_terms(cs::StepIndexCrossSection, λ_pump, T_K; max_iter=20, rtol=1e-10)

Self-consistent guided L01 acoustic mode under the backward-Brillouin Bragg condition, by fixed-point iteration (1–3 steps for SMF-like fibres):

v_eff ← v_core
repeat:
    Ω_B   = 4π·n_eff·v_eff / λ_pump
    V_a   = _acoustic_v_number(a, Ω_B, v_core, v_cladding)
    U_a   = waveguide_factor(V_a)
    β_a   = √((Ω_B / v_core)² − (U_a / a)²)
    v_eff = Ω_B / β_a
until |Δv_eff| / v_eff < rtol

Returns a NamedTuple (v_eff, v_core, v_cladding, V_a, U_a, nu_B, iterations) — velocities in m/s, nu_B (the Brillouin shift) in Hz. λ_pump (m) and T_K (K) must lie in the model validity window; core and cladding must be SilicaGermaniaGlass (so velocity_acoustic applies). Uncertain T_K lifts through Particles — the cutoff and convergence guards reduce over the ensemble extrema.

source
Bifrost.FiberCS._acoustic_v_number — Method
_acoustic_v_number(a, Ω_B, v_core, v_cladding) -> V_a

Acoustic V-number of the L01 mode of a step-index fibre — the acoustic analogue of the optical $V = (2π a / λ)·NA$. a is the core radius (m), Ω_B the acoustic angular frequency (rad/s), v_core/v_cladding the bulk sound velocities (m/s). Requires v_core < v_cladding for every realization so the mode is core-guided.

Signed bend-stress linear birefringence.

λ is the vacuum wavelength in meters, T_K is the temperature in kelvin, and bend_curvature_per_m is the bend curvature κ = 1/R in 1/m (0 for a straight fiber). The value $Δβ$ is in 1/m. The derivative $dΔβ/dω$ is in s/m.

Bifrost.FiberCS._birefringence_bending_coefficient — Method
_birefringence_bending_coefficient(fiber, λ, T_K) -> (C, C_dω)

Curvature-squared coefficient of the bend-stress linear birefringence: $Δβ = C·κ²$, with C in meters (1/m per (1/m)²) and C_dω in s·m.

The whole magnitude is polynomial in the curvature κ — the bend radius enters the underlying stress only as 1/R = κ — so factoring the κ² out lets a caller that also holds the direction of curvature cancel it. The curvature vector's transverse components satisfy k_x² + k_y² = κ² identically, so with (cos 2φ, sin 2φ) = ((k_x²−k_y²)/κ², 2k_xk_y/κ²)

Δβ·cos 2φ = C·(k_x² − k_y²)        Δβ·sin 2φ = C·2k_xk_y

— the form the fiber layer's bend generator uses. See _bend_axis_vector_at.

Bifrost.FiberCS._birefringence_thermal_stress_asymmetric — Method
_birefringence_thermal_stress_asymmetric(fiber, λ, T_K; axis_ratio)
birefringence_thermal_stress_asymmetric(fiber, λ, T_K; axis_ratio)
birefringence_thermal_stress_asymmetric_dω(fiber, λ, T_K; axis_ratio)

Signed linear birefringence from asymmetric frozen-in thermal stress.

λ is the vacuum wavelength in meters, T_K is the temperature in kelvin, and axis_ratio is the dimensionless major/minor core-axis ratio. The value $Δβ$ is in 1/m. The derivative $dΔβ/dω$ is in s/m.

The implemented formula, with ε = axis_ratio and q = 1 − u²/V² (modal_prefactor), is

Δβ = k₀·q·(n³/2)·(p₁₁−p₁₂)·(1+ν)/(1−ν)·(α_clad−α_core)·(T_soft−T)·(ε−1)/(ε+1)

The elastic factor (1+ν)/(1−ν) is the plane-strain elliptical-inclusion result for an isotropic thermal mismatch in an infinite cladding with shared E and ν (Sakai and Kimura, doi:10.1109/JQE.1982.1071451, Eqs. 4 and 29).

Bifrost.FiberCS.aeff — Method
aeff(V; variant = :marcuse_gaussian)

Fundamental-mode effective area normalized to the core radius squared, $A_eff/r₀² = π·(w/r₀)²$, where $w$ is the Gaussian spot size (1/e field radius, equivalently 1/e² intensity). Multiply by r_core(fiber)^2 for the area in square meters, or take r_core(fiber)·sqrt(aeff(V)/π) for $w$.

V is the normalized frequency. variant selects the spot-size convention:

  • :marcuse_gaussian (default) — three-term Marcuse Gaussian approximation $w/r₀ = 0.65 + 1.619/V^{3/2} + 2.879/V^6$ (Marcuse, J. Opt. Soc. Am. 68, 103 (1978), Eq. 15, doi:10.1364/JOSA.68.000103); ~1% for 1.2 ≤ V ≤ 2.4.
  • :petermann — Petermann-II effective spot size, the Marcuse Gaussian form extended by $−(0.016 + 1.561/V^7)$ (Hussey and Martinez, Electron. Lett. 21, 1103 (1985), doi:10.1049/el:19850783); ~1% for 1.5 ≤ V ≤ 2.5.

Pure arithmetic in V, so it lifts through MCM Particles; V is reduced with _mcm_pmin/_mcm_pmax only for the range warnings.

Bifrost.FiberCS.n_shift_stress_thermal — Method
n_shift_stress_thermal(fiber, λ, T_K)

The return value is the common-mode (polarization-averaged) refractive-index shift (dimensionless) at wavelength λ (meters) and temperature T_K (kelvin) from the axisymmetric frozen draw stress set up by the core/cladding thermal-expansion mismatch.

This is the monopole (Δm = 0) companion of the differential [birefringence_thermal_stress_asymmetric] quadrupole: it raises both polarization modes equally rather than splitting them. The implemented formula is

δn = −(n³/4)·q·[(1+ν)(1−2ν)/(1−ν)·(α_core−α_clad)·(T_soft−T)·(p₁₁+p₁₂)
                  + 2·(p₁₂−ν(p₁₁+p₁₂))·(α_core−α_clad)·(T_soft−T)]

where q = 1 − u²/V² is the modal confinement factor (modal_prefactor). The first bracketed term is the plane-strain transverse mean (keeps p₁₁ + p₁₂, no ellipse-asymmetry factor, survives for a circular core); its (1+ν)/(1−ν) is the same plane-strain inclusion factor as in [birefringence_thermal_stress_asymmetric]. The second is the axial photoelastic contribution from the frozen axial stress, with the same p₁₂ − ν(p₁₁ + p₁₂) factor as the applied-tension shift [n_shift_tension_axial].

Approximations carried by this formula (not corrected here):

  • Modal weighting by q = 1 − u²/V² instead of the true guided-mode/stress overlap integral (Steininger et al., doi:10.12688/openreseurope.19414.2).
  • Mean CTE of the cross section approximated as α_clad (a few-percent error).
  • Stresses in the infinite-cladding limit (corrections O((a/R_clad)²)).
  • Uniform E and ν across core and cladding.

Vanishes identically when core and cladding share a thermal-expansion coefficient. Pure arithmetic on material constants, so it lifts through MCM Particles when T_K is.

source
Bifrost.FiberCS.n_shift_tension_axial — Method
n_shift_tension_axial(fiber, λ, T_K; axial_tension_N)

The return value is the common-mode (polarization-averaged) refractive-index shift (dimensionless) at wavelength λ and temperature T_K from a uniform applied axial tension axial_tension_N (N).

Applied tension strains the whole cross section uniformly, so — unlike the bend-coupled differential [birefringence_tension_axial], which scales as 1/R and vanishes on a straight fiber — this isotropic shift is present whether or not the fiber is bent and needs no modal overlap weighting. It is the standard fiber-Bragg-grating strain-optic shift δn = −(n³/2)[p₁₂ − ν(p₁₁ + p₁₂)]·ε_z with axial strain ε_z = F/(π·r_clad²·E) (photoelastic constant p_e = (n²/2)[p₁₂ − ν(p₁₁ + p₁₂)]).

Returns 0 for zero tension. Pure arithmetic on the material constants, so it lifts through MCM Particles when axial_tension_N or T_K is.

Bifrost.FiberCS.β_shift_stress_bend — Method
β_shift_stress_bend(fiber, λ, T_K; bend_curvature_per_m)

Common-mode (polarization-averaged) propagation-constant shift (1/m) at wavelength λ (meters) and temperature T_K (kelvin) for a fiber bent to curvature bend_curvature_per_m = κ = 1/R (1/m; 0 for straight).

This is the monopole (Δm = 0) companion of the differential bend retardance [birefringence_bending]: it raises both polarization modes equally rather than splitting them, so it stays out of the traceless generator and leaves the differential phase and DGD unchanged. It is the second-order effective-index change of the fundamental mode in the conformally transformed index profile of a bent fiber, n(x)·(1 + x/R_eff) (Kiiveri et al., doi:10.1117/1.OE.61.12.126106, Eqs 14 and 20). The combined geometric-curving and elasto-optic effective bend radius is R_eff = R/(1 + k) with k = −(n²/2)·[p₁₂ − ν(p₁₁ + p₁₂)] (Kiiveri Eqs 7 and 18; R_eff ≈ 1.280·R for fused silica). The photoelastic combination p₁₂ − ν(p₁₁ + p₁₂) is the same axial strain-optic factor that sets the applied-tension shift [n_shift_tension_axial].

The tilt is odd in the bend coordinate, so the mode-averaged first-order shift vanishes at the neutral axis; the surviving shift comes from the mode displacing into the higher-index outer side of the bend. A displaced-Gaussian variational estimate with the Marcuse Gaussian fundamental-mode field radius w (the 1/e field radius r_core·sqrt(aeff(V)/π) behind [aeff]) gives

Δβ = k₀³ · n³ · w⁴ / (8 · R_eff²)    (≥ 0).

The 1/8 prefactor is a Gaussian-mode estimate; a rigorous value follows from a full bent-mode solution (Kiiveri §8). The shift scales as (w/R)² relative to β, so it is small for gentle bends and vanishes as R → ∞.

Returns 0 for a straight fiber. Pure arithmetic on the material constants and the Marcuse radius, so it lifts through MCM Particles when T_K is (the bend radius is taken from the Float64 path geometry).
