Nonlinear

Bifrost.Nonlinear.NonlinearGainSpectrum — Type
NonlinearGainSpectrum

Result of a swept-offset nl_gain call: the stimulated modal gain coefficient (W⁻¹m⁻¹) of nonlinear process kind sampled over a grid of frequency offsets Δf at a fixed pump frequency.

Fields

  • kind::Symbol — :raman or :brillouin.
  • f_Hz::Float64 — fixed pump optical frequency (Hz).
  • offset_Hz::AbstractVector — swept offset grid Δf (Hz, positive = Stokes).
  • gain::AbstractVector — modal gain at each offset (W⁻¹m⁻¹); its entries carry Particles when T_K is uncertain.
  • direction::Symbol — Stokes propagation direction (:forward or :back).
  • cross_section::FiberCrossSection — the cross section nl_gain was called with, retained for plot annotation and reproduction. nl_gain is a cross-section quantity (no fibre length or pump power enters), so there is no enclosing Fiber to record here — see NonlinearIntensitySpectrum for the fibre-level counterpart.
  • params::NamedTuple — the keyword arguments forwarded to nl_gain (e.g. T_K, model, fR), retained for reproduction and plot annotation.
source
Bifrost.Nonlinear.NonlinearIntensitySpectrum — Type
NonlinearIntensitySpectrum

Result of a swept-offset nl_intensity call: the single-sided noise power spectral density relative to the pump carrier (dBc/Hz) of nonlinear process kind, sampled over a grid of frequency offsets Δf.

Fields

  • kind::Symbol — :raman or :brillouin.
  • f_Hz::Float64 — fixed pump optical frequency (Hz).
  • offset_Hz::AbstractVector — swept offset grid Δf (Hz, positive = Stokes).
  • psd::AbstractVector — noise PSD at each offset (dBc/Hz); its entries carry Particles when T_K is uncertain.
  • direction::Symbol — Stokes propagation direction (:forward or :back).
  • fiber::Fiber — the fibre nl_intensity was called with, retained for plot annotation and reproduction, with path replaced by nothing (the compiled geometry is the memory-heavy part and plays no role in the spectrum).
  • s::Float64 — observation position along the fibre (m).
  • intensity::Float64 — pump intensity I (W/m²).
  • params::NamedTuple — the keyword arguments forwarded to nl_intensity.

_effective_length_forward(L, α) -> m

Co-propagating effective length L_eff = (1 − e^{−αL}) / α, tending to L as α → 0. α is the power attenuation coefficient (1/m); used for the co-propagating (Raman-Stokes) geometry.

Bifrost.Nonlinear._g_R — Method
_g_R(model::RamanResponseModel, Ω, γ; fR=RAMAN_FRACTION) -> W⁻¹m⁻¹
_g_R(Ω, γ; config=RamanConfig()) -> W⁻¹m⁻¹

Raman modal gain coefficient 2·γ·f_R·Im[h̃_R(Ω)]. Ω is the angular-frequency shift from the pump (rad/s), γ the fibre nonlinear coefficient (W⁻¹m⁻¹). Stokes (Ω > 0) gives positive gain; anti-Stokes (Ω < 0) negative.

The gain is on an absolute scale: Im[h̃_R] is Kramers-Kronig-normalised to Re[h̃_R(0)] = 1, and fR ∈ [0, 1] is the physical fractional Raman contribution to the Kerr response (RAMAN_FRACTION = 0.18 for silica; Agrawal NLFO 6e), not a free scaling factor.

Bifrost.Nonlinear._nl_noise_ratio — Method
_nl_noise_ratio(g_modal, f, Δf, T_K, L_eff, P_pump; quantized, pump_depletion) -> 1/Hz

Single-sided spontaneous-scattering noise power spectral density relative to the pump power, S_noise(Δf)/P_pump. g_modal is the modal gain (W⁻¹m⁻¹), L_eff the effective length (m), P_pump the pump power (W). With quantized, the Stokes band carries (n_th+1) and anti-Stokes n_th; without it, n_th alone. The pump_depletion correction accounts for depletion by this process only, not simultaneous depletion by co- and counter-propagating conversion.

Bifrost.Nonlinear.nl_gain — Method
nl_gain(:brillouin, cs, f, Δf, dir; T_K=297.15) -> W⁻¹m⁻¹

Backward stimulated-Brillouin modal gain coefficient at pump optical frequency f (Hz) and frequency offset Δf (Hz): the Lorentzian material gain brillouin_gain_lorentzian, peaked at the Bragg shift brillouin_freq_shift, divided by the effective mode area. Only backward SBS is modelled — dir must be :back (:forward throws UnsupportedPropertyError) and the core/cladding must be SilicaGermaniaGlass. Uncertain T_K lifts through Particles.

source
Bifrost.Nonlinear.nl_gain — Method
nl_gain(:raman, cs, f, Δf, dir; T_K=297.15, model=:bw, fR=RAMAN_FRACTION) -> W⁻¹m⁻¹

Stimulated-Raman modal gain coefficient at pump optical frequency f (Hz) and frequency offset Δf (Hz, positive = Stokes): 2·γ·f_R·Im[h̃_R(2π·Δf)] with γ the fibre nonlinear coefficient. model selects the response (:bw/:tabulated/:hc or a RamanResponseModel). The Raman gain coefficient is direction-symmetric, so dir (:forward/:back) is validated but does not change the value. Requires a core with a defined Kerr index n₂ (silica / SiGe); a fluorine-doped core surfaces the material layer's UnsupportedPropertyError. Uncertain T_K lifts through Particles.

Bifrost.Nonlinear.thermal_phonon_number — Method
thermal_phonon_number(Ω, T_K) -> dimensionless

Bose-Einstein occupancy n_th(Ω, T) = 1 / (exp(ħ|Ω| / k_B T) − 1) of the phonon band at angular-frequency offset Ω (rad/s) and temperature T_K (K). At the Brillouin band (Ω ≈ 2π·11 GHz, 300 K) n_th ≈ 560; at the Raman peak (2π·13.2 THz) n_th ≈ 0.1. Uncertain T_K lifts through Particles (no per-particle branch — large ħ|Ω|/k_BT sends expm1 to Inf and n_th to 0).
